Roles & Permissions

Permissions are enforced at module, record and action level. Highly sensitive actions carry separate approval permissions.

Permission matrix (summary)

Role Can view Can approve Sensitive access
System Administrator System configuration Access configuration Technical only — not confidential case content
Director / GM Management summaries Selected business decisions Aggregated welfare information only
HR Officer Employee records HR processes Authorised HR and employment records
Operations Manager Sites and deployments Deployment approvals Relevant operational information
Field Supervisor Assigned sites Assigned operational checks Authorised employee information only
Security Guard Own records Own requests Own payslips and support cases
Finance Officer Finance records Assigned finance approvals Salary and payment records
Client Own contracted records Client acknowledgements Own approved reports only
Control Room Operator Operational alerts Assigned incident actions Relevant incident information
Compliance Officer Compliance records Assigned verification Relevant regulatory documents
