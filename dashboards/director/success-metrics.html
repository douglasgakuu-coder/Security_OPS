Success Metrics

How ABC LTD knows VIGILORA is working — measured improvements, not feature counts.

Rule: set targets only after ABC LTD has collected baseline data. This avoids inventing performance targets the company cannot realistically meet.

Measurement principles

  • Measure improvements, not dashboardsOutcome over output
  • Baseline before targeting4–8 weeks of real data
  • Review cadenceMonthly + quarterly deep-dive
  • OwnershipNamed accountable role per metric
  • HonestyReport misses as visibly as wins
