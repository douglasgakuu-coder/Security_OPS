V5 Product Blueprint

Reference view of the VIGILORA V5 platform design — sixteen modules, five core workflows, access rules and the data model that sits underneath.

Design governance: every feature must answer — what problem it solves, who uses it, what action they can take, and how ABC LTD measures whether it works.

Core workflows

  • 1 · Recruitment — vacancy → application → human review → offer → onboarding. No automatic rejection without human review.
  • 2 · Scheduling — overlap blocking, rest validation, conflict check, attendance with correction history.
  • 3 · Welfare complaint — restricted case, authorised officer only; never visible to general supervisors.
  • 4 · Incidents — reference, priority, assignment, action log, authorised closure, full audit history.
  • 5 · Payroll — exception report, finance review, separated approval, published payslips.

Role-based access principles

  • System AdministratorTechnical only — no confidential cases
  • Director / GMAggregated welfare only
  • HR OfficerAuthorised HR records
  • GuardOwn records & payslips
  • ClientOwn contracted records only
  • Control RoomNo payroll access
  • FinancePreparer ≠ approver
