About ABC LTD

Fifteen years of protecting people and property in Kenya

ABC LTD is a Kenyan-owned private security company serving commercial, residential, industrial and institutional clients. Our operations run on VIERRA — a platform that keeps employment records, deployments, attendance, incidents and billing accountable and visible.

🏭

Who we are

A national provider headquartered in Westlands, Nairobi, with branch presence in Mombasa, Kisumu and Nakuru, deploying over 450 trained guards.

🎓

How we train

Induction plus continuous refresher training: customer service, human rights awareness, first aid, occupational safety, incident reporting and privacy awareness.

🏛

How we comply

PSRA registration, Guard Force Numbers, licence renewals and employment documentation are tracked in VIERRA with expiry alerts and corrective actions.

Mission

Better security operations begin with better-managed people
