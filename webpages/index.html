FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are your guards licensed and registered?

Yes. Every guard's PSRA registration and Guard Force Number is tracked in our compliance system, and guards without valid documentation cannot be deployed.

How do you verify attendance and patrols?

Guards clock in from a mobile app capturing GPS coordinates; patrols are verified with QR/NFC checkpoint scans. Missed check-ins trigger automatic supervisor alerts.

What happens if a guard doesn't report for duty?

The system detects the absence, flags the vacant post, and the relief workflow recommends and dispatches an eligible relief guard — quickly and verifiably.

Can clients see their own reports?

Yes. Each client gets an isolated, secure portal showing their own headcounts, patrols, incidents and invoices. Client data is strictly separated.

How is payroll calculated?

Salaries, overtime and Kenyan statutory deductions (NSSF/NHIF) are computed automatically from approved attendance, generating payslips and registers.

Is my data secure?

The platform runs over HTTPS/TLS, encrypts data at rest, uses role-based access control and MFA for privileged accounts, and aligns with Kenya's Data Protection Act (2019).
