Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our guards, our platform, compliance and billing — in plain language.

General

Our services & guards

Are your guards licensed and registered?

Yes. Every guard's PSRA registration and Guard Force Number is tracked in our compliance system, and guards without valid documentation cannot be deployed. Vetting and background verification records are kept current for all personnel.

Which environments do you cover?

Schools and universities, residential estates, commercial buildings, construction sites, hotels, farms and agricultural properties, and industrial facilities. Each site gets its own deployment rules, guard counts and standing orders.

How quickly can deployment begin?

After a structured site assessment and quotation approval, deployment typically begins within 5–10 working days, subject to guard availability and client-specific approval requirements.

Operations

Monitoring & accountability

How do you verify attendance and patrols?

Guards clock in from a mobile app capturing GPS coordinates; patrols are verified with QR/NFC checkpoint scans. Missed check-ins trigger automatic supervisor alerts.

What happens if a guard doesn't report for duty?

The system detects the absence, flags the vacant post, and the relief workflow recommends and dispatches an eligible relief guard — quickly and verifiably, with dispatch and arrival times recorded.

Can clients see their own reports?

Yes. Each client gets an isolated, secure portal showing their own headcounts, patrols, incidents and invoices, with downloadable PDF and Excel reports. Client data is strictly separated.

What happens during a security incident?

The control room acknowledges the alert, verifies it, dispatches a supervisor or emergency service, and records every action with response times. Critical incidents remain open until formally resolved.

Billing & Compliance

Money & regulation

How is payroll calculated?

Salaries, overtime and Kenyan statutory deductions (NSSF/NHIF) are computed automatically from approved attendance records, generating payslips and full payroll registers.

How does client invoicing work?

Invoices are generated monthly per contract, with due dates, payment status tracking and statements available in the client portal. Partial payments are recorded and reconciled.

Is my data secure?

The platform runs over HTTPS/TLS, encrypts data at rest, uses role-based access control and MFA for privileged accounts, and aligns with Kenya's Data Protection Act (2019).
