Employee standards

Welfare is operational, not decorative

Research in the Kenyan private security sector — including the ICoCA Kenya working-conditions study — has highlighted concerns around long shifts, limited rest and delayed pay. We treat those findings as problems to design against, not realities to accept.

Rest and hours

Rest days scheduled and tracked. Overtime is pre-approved and visible in every payslip. Schedules warn against excessive hours.

Transparent pay

Itemised digital payslips with deduction explanations, payment dates and a formal payroll dispute process.

Confidential support

A restricted welfare channel for complaints and support requests — accessible only to authorised case officers, never general supervisors.
